Report a government grievance in your own words.

CitizenVoice reads your complaint, detects how serious and how emotional it is, sets an urgency level, and sends it straight to the responsible agency. Emergencies are escalated at once.

    The agent analyses sentiment and danger signals and assigns High, Medium or Low priority.

  3. 3

    Track

    You get a reference number and can follow the agency's response until it is resolved.
